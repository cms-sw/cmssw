Proposed title: Remove selected offline jet DQM monitor elements

This change applies the 2026-09-30 inventory decisions to the base folders of
cleaned AK4 Calo, PF and PUPPI RECO jet DQM. An empty-by-default disabledMEs
parameter suppresses the selected bookings in each instance. Shared filling
code, jet/event selection, axes and binning are retained.

Implemented scope:
- JetMET/Jet/Cleanedak4CaloJets: 15 general selections, plus 9 under phase2_hgcal.
- JetMET/Jet/Cleanedak4PFJets: 2 general selections, plus 44 under phase2_hgcal.
- JetMET/Jet/Cleanedak4PFJetsPuppi: 30 general selections, plus 5 conditional
  Discard/replace for HGCAL decisions under phase2_hgcal.

The manifest jetDqmCleanupSelection.json records all 100 exact Discard rows
and the 5 separately identified conditional HGCAL decisions. Run 3 disables
47 selections; phase2_hgcal adds 58, for 105 selected names in that context.
These counts describe configured selections, not a general memory reduction.
DiJet/ZJets bookings and the existing PF Uncleaned, CHS, MiniAOD, scouting and
cosmic configurations retain their own monitoring. MC clones of the selected
Calo/PF analyzers use the same sources/base folders and inherit their lists;
the existing MC source does not schedule the RECO PUPPI analyzer.

The RECO/MiniAOD jet-ratio harvester skips booking when its RECO ME is absent,
avoiding null dereferences in customized comparisons. A missing MiniAOD input
alone preserves the prior booking behavior. MET and certification formulas
are unchanged; these pointer guards do not replace retired monitoring.

Monitoring rationale and limits:
- Scouting-specific trigger bookings and Calo JetArea_uncor are not filled by
  the selected RECO implementations. Their uses in preserved modes remain.
- The eight PUPPI energy-fraction profiles and nine PUJetID discriminants are
  not filled by this PUPPI RECO branch. This is not a claim that PUPPI jets
  lack energy fractions, or that MiniAOD uses the same filling branch.
- The three PUPPI Jet-ID pass profiles are populated in the independent
  reference. Their retirement is an explicit monitoring reduction proposal;
  retained cleaned-jet kinematics do not preserve the Jet-ID failure
  denominator. Coverage approval remains outstanding.
- The two general PF BX diagnostics have active conditional filling code.
  They are empty in this sample, which does not prove that their BX gates
  were exercised. Their retirement needs functional review.
- HGCAL-specific endcap distributions include active monitors. This branch
  proposes their era-scoped retirement and supplies no replacement monitor.
  Replacement/retirement coverage and PF Constituents_EndCap certification
  policy must be resolved before an upstream submission.
